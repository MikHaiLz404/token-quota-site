Privacy Policy

Token Quota collects nothing. Everything it reads stays on your Mac.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

No accounts. No servers. No analytics. The developer receives no data about you, your Mac, or your usage. There is no tracking of any kind, and no advertising or analytics SDK is present in the app.

What the app does

Token Quota shows how much of your own AI subscription allowance remains. It reads only what each provider reports, and only for providers you explicitly connect.

Where your data lives

Everything Token Quota holds stays on your Mac: the latest quota reading for each provider you connected, your provider connection settings, and — for connections that need one — a credential stored in the macOS Keychain, reachable only by Token Quota. Nothing is synchronized to iCloud or to any other service.

Providers you connect

Codex

After you grant access to your Codex folder once, Token Quota reads the quota record Codex writes there. No network request is made, and no Codex credential is requested or stored.

Claude Account

You sign in to Claude yourself, in a browser window inside Token Quota that is separate from Safari, Chrome, and Claude Code. Only the resulting Claude session is kept, in Token Quota's Keychain, and it is used only for read-only quota requests to Claude. Token Quota never sends a prompt, never reads another app's credentials, and never accepts a pasted cookie or password.

OpenRouter API Usage

If you choose to add an OpenRouter API key, it is stored in Token Quota's Keychain and used for exactly one read-only request to OpenRouter to retrieve that key's own credit usage.

What we never do

We never send your prompts, files, conversations, or work anywhere. We never read another application's stored credentials. We never estimate a quota you did not have, and we never show an unavailable reading as zero.

Diagnostics

If you choose to export diagnostics, the export is created on your Mac and shared only by you. It deliberately excludes your account identity, credentials, file paths, and raw provider responses.

Purchases

Token Quota Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting a provider removes its stored credential and its local reading. Deleting the app removes everything else Token Quota stored on your Mac.

Children

Token Quota is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
